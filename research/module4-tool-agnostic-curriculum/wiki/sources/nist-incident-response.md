# NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3

Locator: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final

Summary: Incident response is part of continuous risk management, not simply an AI-produced root-cause summary.

- [FACT nist-incident-response] Revision 3 incorporates incident response throughout CSF 2.0 risk-management activities.
- [FACT nist-incident-response] Its stated goals include preparation, reducing incident frequency and impact, and improving detection, response, and recovery.
- [INFERENCE nist-incident-response] A course lab should assess evidence collection, hypothesis testing, containment/recovery decisions, and learning—not whether a specialized SRE agent generated a report.

Limitations: This source gives a comprehensive organizational profile; the course needs a small developer-scale incident exercise.
