# NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

Locator: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/02/nist-releases-version-20-landmark-cybersecurity-framework

Summary: CSF 2.0 supplies an outcome-oriented lifecycle that remains independent of implementation products.

- [FACT nist-csf-2] CSF 2.0 organizes cybersecurity outcomes under Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.
- [FACT nist-csf-2] The framework increased the visibility of governance and supply-chain risk.
- [FACT nist-csf-2] NIST describes outcomes as the “what”; organizations choose the actions that achieve them.
- [INFERENCE nist-csf-2] Outcome-first curriculum design follows the same abstraction: specify what learners must demonstrate before selecting commands or products.

Limitations: CSF is organizational and broad; a short developer course should borrow its lifecycle and governance logic rather than teach the complete framework.
