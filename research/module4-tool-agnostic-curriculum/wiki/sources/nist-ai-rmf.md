# NIST AI Risk Management Framework Core

Locator: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/

Summary: AI governance begins with defined scope, roles, oversight, third-party dependencies, measurement, and documented risk decisions.

- [FACT nist-ai-rmf] The AI RMF calls for differentiating human and AI roles and documenting oversight.
- [FACT nist-ai-rmf] It calls for specifying supported tasks and application scope and mapping risks across third-party software and data.
- [FACT nist-ai-rmf] It treats risk measurement and management as documented, recurring activities rather than a one-time configuration.
- [INFERENCE nist-ai-rmf] A model gateway is only one possible implementation; the durable lesson is an enforceable policy connecting data classes, identities, providers/models, budgets, logs, and approval rules.

Limitations: The AI RMF covers AI systems broadly, while Module 4 focuses on teams consuming coding agents and model services.
