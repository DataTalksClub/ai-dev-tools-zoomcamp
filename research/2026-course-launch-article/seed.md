# AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp 2026 launch article

## Intended reader

Registered learners, people considering joining at launch, and returning learners who know the 2025 edition.

## Desired change

Readers should understand what is new, know exactly where to start, and know where to find authoritative dates, submissions, rules, materials, and help.

## Core claim

The course content changed significantly since the previous launch, but the logistics are essentially the same. The launch article should explain the new course substance while keeping the established Zoomcamp operating model clear and compact.

## Brain dump

The article should put everything a learner needs for the launch in one place. It should link prominently to the course management platform and to the documentation for more information.

Likely form: a practical launch announcement and reference article, concise enough to scan but complete enough to bookmark.

Working assumption: exact deadlines should be linked to the course management platform rather than duplicated in the article, unless the author explicitly wants a dated schedule snapshot.

Open editorial choices:

- Frame the update as “what changed since 2025” or lead directly with the new learning journey.
- Include the full dated module schedule or only the start date plus a platform link.
- Target publication venue and preferred length.

## Must use

- Previous launch/Q&A video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58pn873XO04&list=PL3MmuxUbc_hLuyafXPyhTdbF4s_uNhc43
- AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp docs: https://datatalks.club/docs/courses/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp/
- Current local course repository and 2026 cohort materials.
- Prominent links to the course management platform and the documentation.

## Must avoid

- Do not present the old six-module curriculum as current.
- Do not make stale launch-video details sound current without checking them.
- Do not reproduce large amounts of logistics documentation when a concise summary and canonical link are clearer.

## Constraints

This turn is for an article plan, not a finished article. Preserve the distinction between changed curriculum and unchanged logistics.
